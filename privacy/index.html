1. Plain-English Summary

Welcome to the Privacy Policy for Auralis ("we", "us", "our", or the "Auralis Project"). Auralis is an open-source, community-driven music streaming client, playlist manager, and synchronized lyrics visualizer accessible at https://auralis-music.web.app/ and distributed across web, Android, and desktop platforms.

Our Uncompromising Privacy Commitments

• 100% Free & Open-Source (FOSS): No subscription paywalls, no hidden fees, and no monetization of personal data.
• Zero Third-Party Advertising / Tracking: We run no commercial ad networks (No Google AdSense, No AdMob), no tracking pixels, and zero behavioral telemetry.
• Minimal Data by Architecture: You can use Auralis completely anonymously without signing in. If you sign in, data is used strictly for cross-device library synchronization.
• Strict Read-Only Third-Party APIs: All YouTube access is user-directed, strictly read-only, never cached beyond active playback, never shared, and never used to train machine learning models.
• Zero Data Brokerage: We never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information.

2. Scope & Applicability

This Privacy Policy governs the collection, processing, and storage of information across all distributions of the Auralis software ecosystem, including:

  • The Auralis Web Application and Progressive Web App (PWA) hosted at https://auralis-music.web.app/;
  • The Auralis Android Application (APK) and native mobile packages;
  • Any standalone desktop builds or client utilities distributed via our official GitHub repository (https://github.com/shreyanshchoubey09/Auralis);
  • Associated backend cloud services, including Google Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore synchronization channels.

By accessing or using Auralis, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to the data practices outlined herein. If you do not agree, you should discontinue using Auralis and clear your local client cache.

3. Mandatory YouTube API Services & Google User Data Disclosure

Auralis incorporates and utilizes YouTube API Services to facilitate optional user-directed playlist importation, music discovery, search metadata queries, and streaming playback.

Mandatory Third-Party Agreements

By accessing YouTube-powered capabilities or importing YouTube playlists into Auralis, you explicitly agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Please review the Google Privacy Policy to understand how Google processes and protects your data when interfacing with YouTube API Services.

A. Minimum Scope & Read-Only Limitation

When you authenticate with Google to import your YouTube library, Auralis requests access strictly to the following read-only OAuth 2.0 scope:

https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.readonly

This permission allows Auralis only to retrieve the titles, descriptions, and video lists of your public and unlisted YouTube playlists. Auralis never requests write permissions, channel management rights, private watch/search history, comment privileges, or financial transaction access.

B. Data Storage, AI Training & Sharing Prohibitions

  • No Persistent YouTube Profile Storage: Auralis does not persist your personal YouTube profile credentials or OAuth refresh tokens on central project servers. All tokens reside strictly in client-side secure local storage on your device.
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Google/YouTube user data retrieved through the API is never transferred, disclosed, or sold to external third parties, advertising exchanges, or data brokers.
  • Zero AI Model Training: Under no circumstances is user data derived from YouTube API Services used to train, fine-tune, or develop artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Ephemeral Playback Caching: Track identifiers and metadata imported from YouTube are cached locally in your browser's IndexedDB or personal authenticated Firestore document solely for your individual playback and playlist organization.

C. Revocation of Google & YouTube Permissions

You retain absolute, continuous control over your Google credentials. You may inspect or immediately revoke Auralis’s access to your Google account and YouTube data at any time via the official Google Security Permissions Dashboard:

5. Information We Explicitly DO NOT Collect

To provide complete transparency and eliminate ambiguity, Auralis explicitly declares that we do NOT collect, request, process, or store any of the following sensitive data categories:

Strictly Prohibited & Non-Collected Data

• Audio / Microphone Recordings: Auralis never accesses your microphone, inputs, ambient audio, or voice streams.
• Precise Geolocation Data: We never request GPS, Wi-Fi triangulation, or precise physical location coordinates.
• Biometric Information: We do not collect fingerprint, facial geometry, retina scans, or biometric data.
• Financial & Payment Information: Auralis is 100% free. We never request credit card numbers, bank accounts, or billing addresses.
• Address Book / Contacts: We never access or upload contacts, phone numbers, or social graphs.
• Clipboard / File System: We never scan personal files, documents, photos, or unprompted clipboard contents.

7. Third-Party Sub-processors & External Services

To power cloud synchronization, lyric visualization, and audio resolution without hosting proprietary servers, Auralis integrates with vetted third-party service providers:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.): Provides secure user authentication, Firestore cloud database storage for cross-device playlist syncing, and fast edge-cached web hosting. Certified under ISO 27001, SOC 2, and compliant with EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs). Privacy Policy: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.
  • Google / YouTube API Services (Google LLC): Used to search music metadata, resolve public video identifiers, and import user playlists upon request. Subject to the Google Privacy Policy and YouTube Terms of Service.
  • LRCLIB API: A public, community-maintained open database used to retrieve time-synchronized and plain-text lyrics (`.lrc` files). Queries consist purely of song title and artist strings; no personally identifiable information (PII) or user identifiers are ever transmitted to LRCLIB.
  • GitHub (GitHub, Inc. / Microsoft): Hosts the open-source codebase, release distributions (Android APK binaries), and public issue tracker. Interacting on GitHub is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Local Storage, Caching & Session State

Auralis relies on modern browser storage technologies rather than traditional tracking cookies:

  • IndexedDB: Stores your local music library, downloaded/cached lyrics, album artwork thumbnails, and playback history directly on your physical device for rapid offline loading.
  • LocalStorage: Retains your interface preferences (e.g., dark theme state, visualizer modes, audio volume, repeat/shuffle status).
  • Auth Token Storage: Stores short-lived Firebase Authentication JWT tokens and Google OAuth tokens in secure browser storage to maintain active sign-in status.
  • No Third-Party Advertising Cookies: Auralis does not place tracking cookies, cross-site trackers, or advertising beacons on your browser.

9. "Listen Together" Real-Time Group Playback

The "Listen Together" feature enables multiple users to listen to music in synchronized harmony:

  • Room Architecture: A user creates a room, generating an ephemeral document in Cloud Firestore identified by a random 6-character room code.
  • Data Transmitted: Room state includes the host’s current track, playback progress in milliseconds, play/pause state, and participant display names.
  • Ephemeral Lifespan: Room documents are strictly temporary. When the host leaves or closes the room, all member records are immediately deleted. Inactive rooms are automatically purged after 60 minutes. No chat logs, voice transcripts, or listening histories from shared rooms are archived.

10. Data Retention & Account Deletion Procedures

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the operational purposes described in this policy:

  • Local Device Data: Cleared instantly whenever you clear your browser data/cache, uninstall the application, or click "Reset Library" in Auralis Settings.
  • Cloud Account & Synced Playlists: Retained in Cloud Firestore as long as your account remains active.
  • Self-Service Account Deletion: You can delete individual playlists directly within the app. To request permanent, irreversible deletion of your entire Firebase account profile and associated users/{uid} Firestore data, you may trigger in-app deletion or submit an email to privacy@auralis.app.
  • Deletion Processing Window: Account deletion requests are processed automatically or within thirty (30) calendar days upon receipt.

11. International Data Transfers & Security Measures

Auralis utilizes Google Firebase infrastructure hosted primarily in the United States and global edge locations. For users residing in the European Economic Area (EEA), United Kingdom, or Switzerland:

  • Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs): All cross-border data transfers to Google Cloud servers comply with the European Commission's approved Standard Contractual Clauses (Controller-to-Processor and Processor-to-Processor modules).
  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the Auralis client, Firebase backend, and external APIs are enforced via Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS).
  • Firestore Security Rules: Our Cloud Firestore database is locked down with strict declarative rules ensuring that only authenticated users can read or write their own private document paths (/users/{uid}).

12. Your Global Privacy Rights

Regardless of your geographical location, Auralis upholds comprehensive privacy rights:

A. European Union & UK Residents (GDPR / UK GDPR Rights)

  • Right of Access (Art. 15): You have the right to request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Rectification (Art. 16): You have the right to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.
  • Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Art. 17): You may request complete erasure of your cloud library and account records.
  • Right to Data Portability (Art. 20): You have the right to export your library and playlists in a structured, machine-readable format (e.g., JSON).
  • Right to Restrict or Object to Processing (Arts. 18 & 21): You may object to or restrict certain data processing activities.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3)): You may withdraw your consent for Google/YouTube integration at any moment without penalty.
  • Right to Lodge a Complaint: You have the right to lodge a formal complaint with your national Data Protection Authority (DPA) or Supervisory Authority.

B. California Consumers (CCPA / CPRA Disclosures)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA):

  • No Sale or Sharing of Personal Information: Auralis does not sell and has never sold personal information. Auralis does not share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.
  • Categories Disclosed in Preceding 12 Months: Basic identifiers (Firebase UID, email) and internet activity (playlists, playback state) solely to provide cloud sync and playback services.
  • Right to Non-Discrimination: We will never deny services, degrade quality, or charge different prices for exercising your privacy rights.

13. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & Age Verification)

Auralis is designed and intended for general audiences and is strictly not directed to children under thirteen (13) years of age (or under 16 where required by applicable European Union member state laws).

We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from children under these age thresholds. If you are a parent or legal guardian and discover that your minor child has created an account or submitted personal data without consent, please notify us immediately at privacy@auralis.app. Upon verification, we will promptly purge all associated records from our systems.
